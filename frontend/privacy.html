Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

The Student Success Advisor is a student project (Illinois Tech IPRO 497, Team 3). It is not an official Illinois Tech service, and this is not an official university policy. Nothing the advisor says is an official academic decision.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no logins and no cookies. Nothing ties a conversation to your name.
  • The AI model runs on the project's own computer. Your messages are not sent to outside AI companies.
  • Conversations are deleted after 24 hours without activity. Transcript PDFs are read and then discarded.
  • We never sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or trackers.

What the advisor handles, and for how long

Your messages (up to 4,000 characters each) To understand what you are asking and answer it Stored with your conversation and deleted after 24 hours without activity
Your transcript, if you upload one (PDF, up to 5 MB) To read your completed and in-progress courses, grades and major, so advice fits your record The PDF itself is never saved. The courses, grades and major read from it are kept with your conversation for 24 hours after your last activity

How we protect it

  • Private AI. Every AI request goes through one gateway to a model on the project's own computer, and nowhere else.
  • Personal data filter. Every answer is checked for student IDs, student email addresses, phone numbers, Social Security numbers and birth dates, which are replaced with "[removed]" before you see them.
  • Misuse checks. Messages that try to override the advisor's instructions are blocked and logged, and each part of the advisor may only use its own tools, with limits on what it can send them.
  • Locked-down systems. The database and dashboards accept connections only from the computer running the advisor. Every installation gets its own random passwords, and only the project team can sign in to the dashboards.
  • Automatic deletion. Conversations are deleted after 24 hours without activity and monitoring logs after 7 days, without anyone having to remember.
  • People for important questions. Crisis, academic standing, appeals, financial aid, visa and similar questions are always handed to the right Illinois Tech office or a crisis line instead of being answered by the AI.

Who can see your information

Only members of the project team who can sign in to the monitoring dashboard can see monitoring logs, and they review them to fix errors and look for misuse. We do not share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Your choices

  • You do not have to upload a transcript. You can try a sample student, or tell the advisor your courses yourself.
  • Please do not type your Social Security number, passwords, student ID, or health or financial account details. The filter is a safety net, not a guarantee.
  • To have your conversation deleted before the 24 hours are up, contact the team with the date and time you used the advisor.

Changes to this policy

If how the advisor handles information changes, for example if a different AI model is added, this page will be updated first and its effective date changed.
