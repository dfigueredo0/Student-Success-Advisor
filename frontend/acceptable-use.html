Acceptable Use Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

In a crisis, do not use this advisor. Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), call 911 in an emergency, and on campus also call Public Safety at 312.808.6363.

What the advisor is for

The Student Success Advisor helps Illinois Tech students plan courses: looking up courses, checking prerequisites, and suggesting what to take next, from the public course catalog and your own record. It is a student project (IPRO 497, Team 3), not an official Illinois Tech service.

  • It gives information, not decisions. It cannot approve overrides, overloads, substitutions, exceptions or changes to your record. Only your academic advisor, your department or Academic Affairs can, through an official approval.
  • It can be wrong. Check anything important with your academic advisor or the catalog before you register, drop a course or change your plans.

Please do

  • Use your own information: your own transcript and your own courses.
  • Confirm important answers with your academic advisor.
  • Follow the advisor's suggestion to contact an office when it hands you off.
  • Tell the team if an answer looks wrong or unsafe, so it can be fixed.

Do not

  • Upload or type another person's transcript, grades or personal information.
  • Type your Social Security number, passwords, student ID, or health or financial account details.
  • Try to get around the advisor's safety checks: telling it to ignore its instructions, pretending to be staff, trying to reveal its hidden instructions, or hiding instructions in files you upload.
  • Test the advisor's security, scan it, or try to reach its database or dashboards without the team's written permission. If you find a weakness, please report it instead (see below).
  • Send automated or bulk requests, copy its data in bulk, or try to slow it down or overload it.
  • Present the advisor's answers as an official approval or decision from Illinois Tech.
  • Use it to break Illinois Tech's academic honesty rules or any law, or to harass anyone.
  • Upload files that are not your transcript, or files made to cause harm.

Monitoring

Messages are kept in monitoring logs for up to 7 days, and messages blocked by the safety checks are recorded. The project team reviews these logs to fix errors and spot misuse. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is kept and for how long.

If the rules are broken

Messages that break these rules may be blocked automatically, and requests are rate-limited. The team may restrict access to the advisor, and serious misuse may be raised with the course instructors or the appropriate Illinois Tech office.

Reporting a problem or a security issue

Found a wrong or harmful answer, or a security weakness? Contact the team at [team contact email] with what you did and what you saw. Please do not share a security weakness publicly before the team has had a chance to fix it.
